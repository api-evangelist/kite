When AI agents evolve from “answering questions” to “executing tasks,” payment and authorization become the primary bottlenecks. Traditional credit card processes assume “human-in-the-loop decision-making,” while agents require machine-readable, usage-based billing interfaces with end-to-end auditability. Coinbase’s x402 makes payment a native part of an HTTP request.
